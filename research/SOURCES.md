## The license data as a source

Several statements come from the dashboard's own data, and the entries say so:

- License No. 1 is dated November 12, 1931. Numbers reached about 240 by June 1932.
- Only about 9 percent of licenses numbered before 1940 are still in the public data.
- Batch dates: December through 1978; January to March from 1980; extra batches late in 1988,
  1990 and 1991. This is why 1979, 1989 and 1992 look empty.
- February 1985 batch: 26 licenses (numbers 2707-2733), against 93 in February 1984 and 148 in
  March 1986.
- 71 licenses in 2024 and 62 in 2025, the lowest since 2002.

## Open questions

1. **Text of Chapter 254, Statutes of 1931.** Not located. It would settle what the Board had to
   find before granting the title, and whether there was a grandfathering window.
2. **Were the first titles granted without examination?** The numbering (about 370 in fourteen
   months) suggests most were approved on their record. That is an inference and the entry says so.
   Brandow's slides list a California SE exam from 1931.
3. **1931 or 1932?** Olson, citing a 1981 SEAOC document, says the title was enacted in 1932.
   The Board cites Chapter 254, Statutes of 1931, and the first license is dated November 1931.
   The tab uses 1931 and notes the discrepancy.
4. **STRUCTURE's 2004 article on SE exams** (`str_exam_online_version1.pdf`). Dead link, not
   obtained. The 2004 change to Structural II plus a California exam rests on Brandow's slides
   and the Board's FAQ.
5. **California Regulatory Law Reporter coverage of the April 1988 Special Civil exam and of exam
   scoring in 1985-86.** The Summer 1989 issue on file does not cover either. The 1988 date rests
   on B&P 6755.1 ("on and after April 1, 1988"). The 1985 scoring change rests on Degenkolb and
   the NCEES history, and neither says which California exam was first graded the new way.

### Open questions

1. **No pass rates for the state-written exam before October 2003.** The one figure on file is
   October 2003 (290 tested, 73 passed, 25.17%). The archived index should hold 1999-2002 as well
   and was not transcribed for those years.
2. **Does Degenkolb's "30+% to 10%" refer to the SE exam, and to the exam behind the February 1985
   batch of 26?** He names neither. The CRLR issues for 1984-1987 (Vols. 4-7) are the place to
   check; digital.sandiego.edu and the core.ac.uk mirror both refuse automated fetches.
3. **Sunset reports for 1996-97, 2000-01, 2002-03, 2010-11 and 2014-15.** Not online. They should
   hold multi-year SE pass-rate tables and SE licences issued by fiscal year.
4. **How many candidates completed the exam.** Neither the Board nor NCEES publishes the number
   holding both components (or all four sections). No table on file is a completion rate.
5. **Fall 2017 California results.** Not published anywhere found. Bounded only: above 27% Lateral
   and above 35% Vertical.
6. **Twenty national administrations rest on Godfrey alone.** The NCEES web tables that would
   confirm them are on the Wayback Machine (capture timestamps are listed in the report), which
   could not be fetched. Squared 2014 and 2015 are also missing.
7. **Standalone NCEES results for October 2024, April 2025 and October 2025** by first-time and
   repeat taker. Only the Board's chart values (all takers, buildings) were recovered. The
   October 2025 Lateral Depth pass rate is missing even there.
8. **What changed in April 2026.** Buildings depth first-time rates went from 13-20% to 54-63%
   with an extra hour and, apparently, a standard-setting study in the same window. No source says
   which did the work, or whether the new passing standard applies from April 2026 or April 2027.
9. **SE licences issued in FY 2023/24 and before FY 2015/16, and the exam versus comity split.**
   Not found. DCA annual reports were not opened.
10. **Section-level California results for 2026** and any Board discussion of the April 2026 results.

### Conflicts between sources, and what the report uses

- **Fall 2015 Lateral, California.** 98 tested (archived index) or 68 (Bulletin 50). The report
  uses 98; the sunset report's fiscal-year rate only works with 98.
- **April 2012 Vertical passed.** 62 (standalone page) or 63 (later index). The report uses 62.
- **2024 half-year labels.** Bulletin 77 swaps Lateral and Vertical against the August 2024 and
  February 2025 Board packets. The report uses the packets.
- **Sunset Table 8 candidate counts for FY 2020/21 to 2022/23** do not equal the sums of the
  per-administration counts. The report prefers the per-administration counts.
- **Vertical repeat rate, FY2016-17.** Godfrey gives 23% and 25% for the two sittings; Squared 2017
  prints 32% for the year. Unresolved.
- **Start of the extra hour on depth sections.** October 2025 (Board minutes, May 2025) or spring
  2026 (NCEES memo, SEAOI, NCSEA). The report follows NCEES.
- **End of the state-written exam.** October 2002 plan (CRLR, snippet only) or before 2004
  (Brandow). The Board's October 2003 and October 2004 pages support Brandow.
