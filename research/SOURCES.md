## The license data as a source

Several statements come from the dashboard's own data, and the entries say so:

- License No. 1 is dated November 12, 1931. Numbers reached about 240 by June 1932.
- Only about 9 percent of licenses numbered before 1940 are still in the public data.
- Batch dates: December through 1978; January to March from 1980; extra batches late in 1988,
  1990 and 1991. This is why 1979, 1989 and 1992 look empty.
- February 1985 batch: 26 licenses (numbers 2707-2733), against 93 in February 1984 and 148 in
  March 1986.
- 71 licenses in 2024 and 62 in 2025, the lowest since 2002.

## Open questions

1. **Text of Chapter 254, Statutes of 1931.** Not located. It would settle what the Board had to
   find before granting the title, and whether there was a grandfathering window.
2. **Were the first titles granted without examination?** The numbering (about 370 in fourteen
   months) suggests most were approved on their record. That is an inference and the entry says so.
   Brandow's slides list a California SE exam from 1931.
3. **1931 or 1932?** Olson, citing a 1981 SEAOC document, says the title was enacted in 1932.
   The Board cites Chapter 254, Statutes of 1931, and the first license is dated November 1931.
   The tab uses 1931 and notes the discrepancy.
4. **STRUCTURE's 2004 article on SE exams** (`str_exam_online_version1.pdf`). Dead link, not
   obtained. The 2004 change to Structural II plus a California exam rests on Brandow's slides
   and the Board's FAQ.
5. **California Regulatory Law Reporter coverage of the April 1988 Special Civil exam and of exam
   scoring in 1985-86.** The Summer 1989 issue on file does not cover either. The 1988 date rests
   on B&P 6755.1 ("on and after April 1, 1988"). The 1985 scoring change rests on Degenkolb and
   the NCEES history, and neither says which California exam was first graded the new way.

## Conflicts between sources, and what the tab uses

- **St. Francis Dam deaths.** The Board says 409 to 450; NSPE-California says over 600. The tab
  quotes the Board.
- **Field's name.** "Charles Field" (SEAONC), "Don C. Field" (Olson, Seismic Safety Commission).
  The tab uses Don C. Field.
- **Statewide SEAOC founding.** 1931 (SEAOSC page) or 1932 (Olson, citing Binder). The tab gives
  only the December 1932 first convention.
